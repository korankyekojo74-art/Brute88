ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

LYR XIX / Brute88.com — Updated Oct 22, 2026

LYR XIX is committed to making brute88.com accessible to everyone, including people who are blind, use screen readers, or navigate with keyboard only. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

WHAT WE DO

• Alt text for all product images
• All functions work with keyboard only (Tab, Enter)
• High color contrast and resizable text to 200%
• Semantic HTML: H1, headings, landmarks, labels
• Clear text instructions for CashApp checkout
• Visible focus outline on all links

NEED HELP?

We test with VoiceOver (iPhone), TalkBack (Android), and keyboard-only navigation. If you cannot use any part of brute88.com, contact us and we will provide an accessible alternative within 24 hours and fix the barrier within 48 hours.
