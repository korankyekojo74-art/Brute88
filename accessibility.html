ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

LYR XIX / Brute88.com — Updated Oct 22, 2026

LYR XIX is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people who are blind, use a screen reader, or navigate with a keyboard only.

WHAT WE DO

• We use alt text for all product images
• All functions work with keyboard only (Tab, Enter)
• We maintain high color contrast and resizable text
• We use semantic HTML: headings, labels, and landmarks
• Our CashApp checkout includes clear text instructions, not just images

STANDARDS

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

NEED HELP?

If you cannot use any part of brute88.com with a screen reader or keyboard, contact us and we will assist you directly and fix it within 48 hours.
